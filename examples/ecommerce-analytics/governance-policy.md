## 6. Legal Basis (LGPD)

| Data | Legal basis | Article |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Customer data for sales analysis and operations | Execution of purchase and sale contract | Art. 7, V |
| Behavioral analysis for marketing campaigns | Legitimate interest — prevent cancellations and personalize offers | Art. 7, IX |

> DPO: the company does not yet have a formal DPO. Recommended to hire or designate a responsible party as the volume of personal data processed grows.
