# Data Product Brief
**Project:** Sales Analytics — Sample Store
**Date:** 2024-03-01
**Business owner:** Carolina Mendes — Head of Commercial
**Status:** Approved

> 📝 *Artifact generated by the **Data Product Strategist** persona after an interview with the Head of Commercial.*

---

## 1. Business Problem

The Sample Store commercial team makes campaign and weekly target decisions based on reports manually exported from the ERP every Monday morning. The process takes ~3 hours, is done by one analyst, and the data is already stale by the time it arrives. Weekend campaigns are only evaluated the following week. Twice in the last 6 months the report had errors due to a corrupted spreadsheet, causing rework and wrong decisions about commissions.

## 2. Decisions this data will enable

- **Decision 1:** Adjustment of weekly targets per salesperson based on real performance (today done with D-7 data)
- **Decision 2:** Evaluation of promotional campaigns within 24h of closing (today D+7)
- **Decision 3:** Identification of customers at risk of churn for proactive action by the CS team (currently does not exist)
- **Decision 4:** Automated monthly commission calculation per salesperson (today manual, ~1 day of work)

## 3. Consumers

| Consumer | Usage type | Tool | Access frequency |
|----------|------------|------|------------------|
| Commercial team (8 people) | Target and performance tracking | Metabase | Daily |
| Head of Commercial (Carolina) | Strategic decisions and campaigns | Metabase | Daily |
| CS team (5 people) | Identifying at-risk customers | Metabase | 3x per week |
| Finance | Commission calculation | CSV export / Metabase | Monthly |

## 4. Refresh requirements

- **Frequency:** Daily
- **Tolerance window:** Data for day D available by 07:00 on day D+1 (before the start of the business day)
- **Critical time:** 07:00 — morning report the commercial team accesses upon arrival

## 5. Estimated volume

- **Orders per day:** ~2,000 orders (peak of ~8,000 on special dates)
- **Active customers:** ~150,000
- **Expected growth:** 15% per year
- **Seasonality:** Black Friday (10x volume), Mother's/Father's Day (4x), end of month (2x)

## 6. Data sources

| Source | Type | Owner | Access available? | Known quality? |
|--------|------|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| ERP PostgreSQL (Tiny) | Relational database | IT team (Bruno Costa) | Yes — read-only already configured | Good. Occasional duplicates in `orders` due to a known ERP bug |
| Payment gateway API (PagSeguro) | REST API | Finance (Marcos Lima) | Pending approval — 5 business days | Unknown to the team |
| Salesperson spreadsheet | Google Sheets | HR (Paula Ferreira) | Yes | Manual — may have name inconsistencies |

## 7. Constraints and risks

- **Privacy/GDPR:** Customer SSN, email, and phone present in the ERP — anonymization required before any analytical layer
- **Access:** PagSeguro API approval still pending — may delay financial reconciliation stories
- **Source quality:** ERP duplicate bug documented by IT team — dedup workaround required
- **Deadline:** First delivery (basic sales dashboard) expected in 4 weeks
- **Infrastructure:** Company uses GCP — BigQuery available but no data dataset created yet

## 8. Success criteria

> "The commercial team can access prior-day sales performance in Metabase by 07:00, without manual intervention, with data reconciled against the ERP. The analyst who currently does the manual report can validate that the numbers match."

## 9. What is OUT of scope

- Predictive churn analysis (phase 2 — post base pipeline validation)
- CRM integration (Salesforce) — pending contract definition with vendor
- Historical data prior to 01/01/2023 — insufficient quality for reliable analysis
- Real-time / streaming — daily batch is sufficient for all identified use cases

## 10. Notes and observations

⚠️ **Identified warning sign:** The salesperson spreadsheet in Google Sheets is maintained manually by HR. Salesperson names may have variations (e.g. "John Smith" vs "John S."). Recommend normalizing before the build or handling it in Silver.

⚠️ **Identified warning sign:** "Churn" does not yet have a formal definition at the company. Before building the at-risk customers feature, align the definition with CS and Commercial (e.g. customer without a purchase for X days). Defer to phase 2.
